# Monopoly lens
Score: 7/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Hyper-Local Density & Proprietary Tech Bridge:** National giants (Shopee, Tokopedia, OLX) cannot economically solve 500-meter campus deliveries or run personalized WhatsApp bots tailored to USU/Polmed subcultures.
- **Local Network Effects:** Every new student seller brings inventory that attracts campus buyers; every student buyer makes posting listings more attractive. This is a classic two-sided micro-monopoly within a 3 km radius.
- **Brand Resonance:** "Kampus Kita / Jual Beli USU Polmed" owns the exact organic search and student mindshare that corporate classifieds can never duplicate.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Platform Fragility (Meta / WhatsApp Ban Risk):** Baileys-based unofficial WhatsApp automation carries constant risk of account bans or protocol breakages if anti-spam thresholds are crossed.
2. **Low Barriers to Entry from Free Chat Groups:** Unorganized Telegram groups or Instagram Menfess accounts provide a rough substitute for free, even if disorganized.
3. **Failure to Monopolize the Critical Cohort:** If graduating seniors leave and incoming freshmen are not acquired in week 1 of the new academic semester, liquidity evaporates every September.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is your proprietary data moat? Do you track real campus pricing history, student reputation scores, or verified faculty credentials?
- How quickly can you replicate this exact playbook to UNIMED, UINSU, and IT Del to prevent a regional copycat?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- The database must own the verified student identity graph (NIM / campus email / phone verification), making trust portable across semesters.
- WhatsApp bot must have strict rate-limiting, session recovery watchdog, and multi-tenant failover architecture to survive Meta updates.
