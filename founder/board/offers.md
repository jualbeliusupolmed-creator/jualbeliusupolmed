# Offers lens
Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Immediate Dream Outcome for Sellers:** A student with a second-hand fan or textbook wants it sold *today* to someone within walking distance so they can pay for dinner or printing credits. Time delay to sale is hours, not days.
- **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement:** High because WhatsApp is where Indonesian students live 24/7. When a listing gets pushed to a targeted campus broadcast list, response rates are 10x higher than cold web search.
- **Near-Zero Effort & Sacrifice for Buyers:** No shipping costs, no waiting 3-5 days for JNE/J&T, no packaging hassle. The deal happens at Biro Rektor or front of faculty canteen.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Low Willingness-to-Pay for Casual Sellers:** Casual students selling a single Rp 30.000 lab coat might resist paying Rp 5.000 for a boost unless the speed difference is visibly guaranteed.
2. **Ghosting and Flaking on COD:** If buyers ghost or cancel at the meeting spot, the seller's perceived value crashes, blaming the platform.
3. **Offer Margin Leakage to Off-Platform Channels:** Once a buyer and seller connect, they take all repeat deals to personal WhatsApp contacts, bypassing platform monetization.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What specific guarantee can you bundle with the Rp 5.000 boost (e.g. "Sold in 48 hours or free repost + refund")?
- Can you bundle high-ticket student needs (kost deposits, semester textbook bundles, motorbike rentals) where a 5% fee is worth real money?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Create an irresistible "Lapak Sultan" bundle: for Rp 15.000, give 3 boosts, a verified green checkmark, and an auto-bump every 12 hours.
- Implement an automated escrow or deposit commitment feature for high-value items so neither party flakes.
