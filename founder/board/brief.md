# Board Brief: Kampus Kita (Jual Beli USU Polmed)

## Executive Summary
Kampus Kita is an active, functioning hyper-local student marketplace operating across Universitas Sumatera Utara (USU) and Politeknik Negeri Medan (Polmed). Unlike generic e-commerce platforms (Shopee, Tokopedia) or classifieds (OLX, FB Marketplace), Kampus Kita solves the immediate physical proximity problem of campus life: instant COD (Cash-on-Delivery), zero delivery fees, zero shipping delays, and trusted peer transactions verified via student identities.

## Tech & Operational Reality
- **Platform:** Web portal (`kampuskita.my.id`), WhatsApp Bot (`wa-bot-usu` on Azure VPS), Instagram DM automated bot.
- **Traction Base:** Actively serving campus traffic in Medan with automated Baileys transport bridge, outbox workers, and student moderation queue.
- **Revenue Model:** Micro-monetization via Promoted WhatsApp Blasts (Rp 5.000), Monthly Verified Seller Passports (Rp 15.000), and Open PO transaction fees (5%).
- **Constraints:** Must operate with low human moderation overhead, zero fraudulent COD risks, and withstand WhatsApp session resets or Meta webhook changes.
