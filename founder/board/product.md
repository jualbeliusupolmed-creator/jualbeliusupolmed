# Product lens
Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Frictionless Chat-First Experience:** Students do not want to download another 150MB mobile app from the Play Store or fill out 10-step forms. Posting via WhatsApp or DM Instagram with automated parsing is clean, intuitive, and respectful of the user's time.
- **Speed to First Contact:** Clean web interface (`kampuskita.my.id`) with instant direct WhatsApp chat buttons eliminates cumbersome in-app messaging barriers.
- **Architectural Discipline:** Clear separation between transport bridges (Baileys/Instagram webhook) and core logic (Supabase database, Next.js server actions).

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Design & Experience Degradation:** If the web UI looks like a sloppy template, spam-infested board, or broken webview, trust instantly evaporates.
2. **Slow Bot Response / Latency:** If WhatsApp messages hang or take >30 seconds to confirm listings, students will assume the service is dead.
3. **Spam & Moderation Leakage:** Inappropriate content, loan sharks (pinjol), fake merchandise, or predatory ads slipping into campus broadcast groups will ruin the community reputation.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Is the listing flow truly instantaneous? Can a student post a textbook from photo snap to published URL in under 45 seconds?
- How does the product feel? Does it embody student craft and dignity, or does it look like an ad-heavy spam directory?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Adopt a strict, uncompromising design standard (Hallmark style): zero visual slop, ultra-fast loading, clear typography, and zero fake engagement numbers.
- Automated moderation pipeline with keyword filters, duplicate image detection, and 1-click ban for spam numbers.
