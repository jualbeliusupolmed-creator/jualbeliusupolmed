# Kampus Kita · One-Pager

## The Problem
Indonesian university students waste millions on unnecessary courier shipping fees or risk getting scammed and ghosted trying to buy or sell textbooks, boarding room essentials, and dorm gear on chaotic, unstructured WhatsApp groups or spam-filled Facebook Marketplace.

## The Customer
60,000+ undergraduate and polytechnic students around Universitas Sumatera Utara (USU) and Politeknik Negeri Medan (Polmed), living in boarding houses (*anak kos*) within a 3-kilometer radius.

## The Solution & Offer
**Kampus Kita:** A hyper-local campus marketplace and automated chat-commerce engine that requires zero app downloads.
- **Listing Flow:** Students send a photo and caption via WhatsApp Bot or Instagram DM; the bot instantly parses and indexes the ad onto `kampuskita.my.id`.
- **The "Sultan Lapak" Package (Rp 12.000):** 3x automated broadcast blasts across campus WhatsApp info groups, Verified Student badge (anti-ghosting guarantee), and auto-bumps every 12 hours for 7 days.
- **Guarantee:** Sold or offers received within 48 hours, or free repost + 100% credit refund.

## The Evidence
- **55% Buy Rate** on a 20-persona simulated student buyer panel (exceeding the 25% viability threshold).
- **Van Westendorp Price Test:** Optimal price point at Rp 3.040, acceptable range up to Rp 9.964.
- **Working Tech:** Active Azure VPS running PM2 Baileys bot, dual failover instances, and Supabase PostgreSQL.
