## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, structured academic commerce and student community ecosystem tailored specifically for collegiate networks (USU & POLMED). It blends the rigor and credibility of an institutional identity with the nimble, hyper-local nature of student-driven group orders (Open PO) and peer-to-peer campus marketplace dynamics.

The aesthetic follows an **Academic-Tech Minimalist** movement:
- **Clean Structure & Hyper-Legibility:** Deep emerald and evergreen foundational accents convey institutional gravity, accountability, and financial security, countering the typical chaos of unverified social media commerce.
- **Micro-Verification & Provenance:** Visual emphasis is placed on student authenticity markers (verified NIM/Stambuk badges, faculty affiliations, and hyper-local pickup landmarks such as pintu gerbang, pendopo, and kantin).
- **Urgency without Alarmism:** Pre-order deadlines, batch completion bars, and remaining slots leverage focused warm amber indicators, while community-driven menfess tags and campus announcements lean into vivid collegiate violet accents.
- **Physical Campus Integration:** High-density, scannable data layouts designed for on-the-go student movement across sprawling campus grounds under direct sunlight.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high contrast, institutional authenticity, and distinct semantic cues for university life.

- **Primary (`#047857` Emerald Green / `#064E3B` Deep Forest):** Represents official campus authority, completed orders, verified stambuk status, and core transactional calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#7C3AED` Collegiate Violet):** Designates social menfess discussions, peer-to-peer community questions, anonymous voice threads, and campus association badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` Active Amber):** Reserved strictly for temporal urgency: batch cut-off countdowns, remaining slot quotas ("Tersisa 3 slot"), and impending COD meet-up alerts.
- **Neutral Palette (`#0F172A` Slate Dark, `#64748B` Slate Muted, `#E2E8F0` Slate Border, `#F8FAF9` Canvas Warm White, `#FFFFFF` Surface White):** Delivers clean contrast against North Sumatra's bright outdoor lighting, avoiding cold sterile grays in favor of slightly warmed mineral slate tones.

### Semantic Mapping
- **Surface Base:** `#F8FAF9`
- **Surface Container (Card / Sheet):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Highlight / Subtle Tint:** `#ECFDF5` (Emerald tint for verified states), `#F5F3FF` (Violet tint for community states), `#FFFBEB` (Amber tint for pending/quota states)
- **Text Primary:** `#0F172A`
- **Text Secondary / Metadata:** `#475569`
- **Border / Divider:** `#E2E8F0`

## Typography

The typography couples the modern, geometric friendliness of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the structural legibility of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean authority in item listings, campaign targets, campus milestone titles, and batch names. Letter tracking is set slightly tight (-0.02em) on headlines above 20px.
- **Body & Product Descriptions (Inter):** Maximizes readability for detailed pre-order guidelines, payment terms, and menfess message narratives across compact viewport widths.
- **Labels, Badges, & Indicators (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Styled with high-contrast semi-bold and bold weights to ensure verification pills (e.g., `FASILKOM-TI '21`, `POLMED REKAYASA '22`) and batch quota numbers are immediately scannable in dense feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

The ecosystem operates on an adaptive fluid grid with a strict 4px/8px incremental spacing rhythm:

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column feed with a 4-column sub-grid, 16px margins, and 16px gutters. Optimized for rapid single-thumb interaction, sticky bottom checkout/order trays, and continuous scroll list streams.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px margins. Splits into dual columns (e.g., active PO item feed paired with landmark map selection / menfess real-time ticker).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at 1280px total container width, 24px gutters, and 48px margins. Employs a three-pane structure: Navigation and Faculty Filters (col 1–3), Core Feed / Batch Explorer (col 4–9), and Landmark COD Hub / Active Orders Drawer (col 10–12).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, clean surface separations rather than heavy blurred drop shadows to retain high performance and clarity.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAF9` surface background for overall page wrapper.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Outline & Planar Separation):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a sharp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Used for standard feed cards, menfess posts, and student profile info panels.
- **Level 2 (Active Interactive Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with a directional micro-shadow: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Applied to active PO campaigns and hover states.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navigation & Verification Flyouts):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04)`. A subtle forest-green ambient diffusion ensures floating modals, pickup point sheets, and bottom sticky bars feel anchored to the campus theme.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2` / 8px default, 16px for cards, and full pill shapes for identity badges):

- **Badges and Metadata Pills:** Full circle/capsule radii (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for student verification tags, campus faculties, stambuk years, and status chips.
- **Cards and Containers:** 16px (`rounded-lg`) corner radii, balancing soft tactile appeal with efficient content bounds.
- **Buttons and Inputs:** 8px (`rounded-base`) for clear, actionable click targets that maintain precise structure.
- **Progress Bars:** Fully rounded capsule tracks and fills to signify batch quota accumulation smoothly.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Order Button:** `#047857` background, white label (`label-lg`), 8px border radius, 12px 20px padding. Hover state deepens to `#064E3B`. Focused state includes a 3px outline in `#A7F3D0`.
- **Secondary (Menfess/Community):** `#F5F3FF` surface, `#7C3AED` text, 1px border in `#DDD6FE`. Hover transitions to `#EDE9FE`.
- **Urgent Action (Join PO Quota):** `#F59E0B` solid background with dark slate `#0F172A` text to emphasize immediate deadlines.

### Verification Chips & Identity Badges
- **Stambuk & Faculty Badge:** Capsule pill (`rounded-full`), padding 4px 10px. `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` text and a subtle verified check icon. Example: `USU • FASILKOM '21`.
- **Institutional Marker (POLMED):** Capsule pill with `#EFF6FF` background and `#1D4ED8` text for cross-campus clarity.
- **Menfess Topic Chip:** `#F3E8FF` background with `#6B21A8` text, prefixed with a campus hash tag.

### Cards
- **Pre-Order (Open PO) Card:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, 16px radius, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Features a two-tiered header: Organizer info + Verified Badge on the left, Countdown Timer chip (`#FFFBEB` with `#B45309` text) on the right. Includes a linear quota bar (`#E2E8F0` track, `#047857` progress fill) displaying "Slot: 18/25 Terkumpul".
- **Landmark Pickup Selector Card:** Compact 12px padded card showcasing COD points (e.g., *Pendopo USU, Pintu 1 Padang Bulan, Kantin Gedung Z POLMED*), accompanied by a map pin and scheduled pickup hour.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search & Filter Input:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, 8px radius, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Placeholder text `#94A3B8`. Active focus rings shift border to `#047857` with 3px `#ECFDF5` glow.
- **Batch Slot Counter:** Segmented stepper control bounded by `#E2E8F0`, allowing quick increment/decrement of ordered units.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Menfess Stream Item:** Border-bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`, zero card elevation for high-density reading. Violet accent bar (2px) indicates high-engagement campus topics.
